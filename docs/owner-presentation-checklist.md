# Club Bahia Growth OS — Owner Presentation Checklist

## Goal

Present the system as a practical operating tool for saving staff time, coordinating promotion, and connecting promotion to reservation outcomes. Do not present optional creative tools or unactivated social integrations as the main product.

## Before the meeting

1. Confirm the latest deployment is Ready.
2. Sign into the Growth OS once with the owner account.
3. Open `/admin/overview` in the browser.
4. Confirm Media opens without a second asset password.
5. Choose one real upcoming Club Bahia event with accurate details.
6. Confirm at least one approved image or finished vertical video is available.
7. Remove test events or reservation requests that would distract from the demonstration.
8. Keep real provider publishing switches off unless the Club Bahia accounts have already passed supervised validation.

## Five-minute walkthrough

### 1. Owner overview

Explain the operating loop:

- enter event facts once;
- generate platform-specific promotion;
- reuse approved media;
- review blockers and approvals;
- schedule or record publication;
- follow up with guests;
- review reservation results.

### 2. Events

Open one real event and show that the date, performer, genres, admission, age policy, reservation link, and other facts act as the source of truth.

### 3. Promotion

Open Create Promotion and show the channel-specific package. Emphasize that AI drafts content, while deterministic checks and staff approval control what moves forward.

### 4. Media

Open the global Media Library and then the event Choose Media step. Emphasize:

- no separate media password after staff sign-in;
- approved files can be reused without another upload;
- rights, credits, tags, and usage history stay attached to the asset;
- advanced crops and branded graphics are optional.

### 5. Review inbox

Show how staff can review multiple events from one screen, assign approved media, fix blockers, and approve only items that currently pass the safety gates.

### 6. Guests and results

Show the Guest Follow-Up Queue, reminder presets, reviewed text/email templates, and event Results page. Explain that guest communication remains manual and that the system does not invent social metrics.

## What is ready now

- Event planning and recurring templates
- Campaign generation and quality checks
- Reusable media and event assignments
- Cross-event promotion review
- Reservation intake and follow-up reminders
- Campaign attribution and event results
- Production activation checklist

## What still requires activation

- Stable production-domain confirmation
- Real Meta account connection and supervised publishing proof
- TikTok connection and permitted publishing validation
- Protected recurring scheduler appropriate to the Vercel plan
- Real provider analytics permissions

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim that the system currently:

- publishes unattended to every social platform;
- automatically messages reservation guests;
- renders finished multi-clip MP4 videos;
- guarantees platform-safe crops for every future interface change;
- measures reach or watch time without provider analytics access.

## Recommended closing

End with one concrete proposal: run one upcoming event through the full workflow, measure staff time saved and reservation response, then activate additional automation only where the real pilot proves it is useful.
